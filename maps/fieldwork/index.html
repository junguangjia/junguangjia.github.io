Sources, precision & interpretation

China: 15 editable segments digitized from a supplied hand-drawn Amap screenshot, then registered to geographic coordinates. Colors in the original sketch do not establish a transport mode, trip count or travel direction. Town labels on the basemap are geographic references, not an added visit list.

Mongolia: Ulaanbaatar and the Yanran Inscription are the supplied endpoints. The connecting line is schematic. No intermediate towns have been invented. The monument location is rounded for public display.

South Korea: 17 supplied inscription entries are grouped into 13 city/county areas. Markers locate those areas, not exact monument positions or current museum holdings. Individual visit completion and itinerary order still require confirmation, so no route order is asserted.

Japan: five named cities are shown. Dashed links organize them geographically; they are not a dated itinerary or a transport track.

Counts: the supplied screenshot reports 210 cities in China. It does not establish a worldwide total. This atlas is a selected overview, not the complete 1,300+ inscription collection.

Cartography: geographic shapes come from Natural Earth (10m land; 50m boundary lines), clipped to the region and simplified for web display, never redrawn by an image model. The map uses Web Mercator. Boundaries are for geographic reference and do not express a position on sovereignty.
