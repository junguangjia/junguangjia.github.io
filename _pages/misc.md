## Solo cycling expedition to Lhasa

In 2025, I cycled solo from Pingdingshan, Henan, to Lhasa, Tibet — 3,680 km (2,286 miles) in 51 days, crossing 4 provinces, 16 cities, and 15 major mountain sections before reaching the Tibetan Plateau. More than the distance itself, the journey taught me how much persistence can come from curiosity, and how much a long journey can be shaped by the kindness of people encountered along the way.

## Field documentation for ArtVenn

During my gap year, I traveled through 200+ cities across China and made additional field trips to Mongolia, Japan, and South Korea, documenting more than 1,300 historical inscriptions and cliff carvings and traveling roughly 54,000 km (33,500 miles). I collected photographs, geographic coordinates, and contextual records to build the primary-source collection behind ArtVenn, with the goal of preserving these materials in a structured form for documentation, retrieval, and future research.

{% include fieldwork-map.html %}

These experiences reflect a broader interest of mine: following questions far enough to understand them firsthand, whether through research, fieldwork, or simply going somewhere I have never been before.
